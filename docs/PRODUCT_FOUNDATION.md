# Locked product foundation

Milo & Me is a child's virtual best friend, growing through creativity, curiosity, play, learning and everyday adventures. The pet is the emotional centre and interface. Activities are things the child and pet do together.

**Play → Curiosity → Interaction → Discovery → Learning.** Every feature must strengthen the relationship, be enjoyable as play, offer creative/reasoning/imagination value and earn parental trust. Optimize meaningful engagement and healthy endings rather than screen time.

## Audience and experience

Ages approximately 5–8; parents own sensitive decisions. Large touch targets, minimal reading, visual/tap alternatives, useful narration, predictable navigation, calm motion, responsive phones/tablets and accessible contrast. Warm, illustrated, premium storybook design: cream, sage, sky, peach, lavender and warm yellow. Use reproducible Flutter/Flame assets; replace temporary art through explicit interfaces.

The child's home is a room: easel for art, book for stories, puzzle box for play, wardrobe for outfits, shelf for memories. The pet reacts, occasionally suggests activities and remembers safe local experiences. Never expose a statistics dashboard for pet care.

## V1 scope

- One original pet; naming, limited appearance customization, expressions, emotional reactions, local memory, room changes and day/night awareness.
- Gentle feeding, washing, resting and affection, without absence penalties.
- Drawing: Watch, Draw with Me and Create; strokes, guide playback, undo/redo, brushes, eraser, saving and artwork visible in the world. Architecture target: about 24 lessons.
- Reusable matching, sorting, sequencing, spatial and logic puzzles. Architecture target: 50–60 levels.
- Data-driven branching stories with choices, rewards and resume. Architecture target: 10–12 stories.
- Wardrobe and cosmetic rewards, memory scrapbook, local profile, content packs, accessibility, sound settings and parent session controls.
- Parent-owned accounts, verified consent where required, entitlements, versioned downloads and optional backup architecture. Paid features remain behind the parent gate.

The subsequently approved `EXECUTION_V02_V07.md` extends this V1 baseline through six world areas, virtual cooking, structured roleplay, safe science, parent services and premium architecture. These extensions preserve the north star; unrestricted AI conversation and child cloud accounts remain excluded.

## Trust rules

No ads, tracking for advertising, loot boxes, stranger/public chat, child leaderboards, manipulative streaks, scarcity, paid energy or child-facing purchases. Never guilt a child for leaving. Do not collect a child's email, phone, precise location, contacts or recordings. Local-first play and creations must survive network failure.
